# whynot — privacy policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

whynot is a hackathon project (Meta Global AI Developer Hackathon, 2026) by Matteo Atkinson and Tom Angles. It prepares Meta ad campaigns for small businesses and publishes them only with the business owner's approval.

## What whynot reads
- Public App Store information about the app being advertised (description, rating, public reviews, screenshots). Reviewer names are not kept.
- Public ads in the Meta Ad Library (ad text, dates, reach by age, gender and country).
- For a business that connects its ad account: the ad account's campaigns and their results, with the owner's written consent.

## What whynot does with it
- Writes ad strategy and ads, tests them in a simulated feed, and prepares a campaign plan.
- Publishes ads to a Meta ad account only after the owner approves, within a daily budget the owner sets. Everything is created paused first.

## What whynot never does
- It does not collect data about the people who see the ads, and does not build audiences of individuals.
- It does not sell or share data. Business data stays on the machine that runs whynot and is not published.
- It does not keep access tokens in logs or files other than the operator's local configuration.

## Data deletion
To have any data about your business deleted, contact the authors through the project repository: https://github.com/Chocolatine75/whynot (open an issue or contact the maintainers). We delete it within 30 days.
