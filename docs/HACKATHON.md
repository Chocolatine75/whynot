## Problem statements (project must fit one)

- **Build for Your World**: bring Meta's models into your domain, solve a problem you know firsthand. ← **ours**
- Push the Frontier: software, robotics, manufacturing, energy, science.

## Allowed Meta tools

Muse Spark series, Muse Wonder, Muse Image, Muse Voice Transcribe, SAM 3.1 (Meta Model API); open weights Muse Glimmer and Muse Spark Open; Muse Code coding agent.

## Rules

- Repository must be **public**.
- **Demo must only show what we built during the hackathon.** Judges must be able to tell what was created during the event, otherwise disqualification.
- **New work only.**
- Disqualified if the project violates legal, ethical or platform policies, or **uses code, data or assets we don't have the rights to**.
- Anti-projects to avoid: medical advice, basic RAG, simple image analyzers, edu chatbots, job screeners, nutrition coaches, personality analyzers, sports coaches, political/war. We are clear of all of these.

## Submission

- One submission per team, by the team Representative, on the Cerebral Valley platform.
- Public repo link, **3-minute demo video**, all team members listed.
- The video, the code and the live product are the main judging artefacts.

## Judging

1. **Stage 1 (pass/fail):** fits a theme and genuinely uses Meta models.
2. **Stage 2 (0–10 per criterion):**
   - **Impact 30%**: real-world potential, who benefits, could people actually use it.
   - **Use of Meta AI tooling 25%**: creative, beyond basic integration, capabilities that surprise Meta.
   - **Technical depth & execution 20%**: sound, refined engineering, real craft.
   - **Demo 25%**: working, impressive, holds up live, video genuinely cool to watch.
3. **Stage 3:** top 5 per track ranked live on the same rubric.
