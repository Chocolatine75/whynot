# Demo script (3 minutes)

The video is a primary judging artefact. Show only what we built during the hackathon. Every number on screen is real and measured; anything simulated is labelled.

## 0:00–0:20 — Hook
Two shoppers looked at the same jacket. One left after opening the size guide. One left at the shipping page. Today they get the same retargeting ad. Ad budget is wasted on the wrong message, and on people who'd have bought anyway.

## 0:20–0:50 — Connect & understand
Install on the partner's Shopify store. Live:
- competitor ads pulled from the Meta Ad Library, with who they actually reach;
- Muse Wonder's strategy brief appears;
- the store's objection map: "38% sizing, 24% shipping, 15% trust…".

## 0:50–1:40 — Generate
- ~10 templates appear, each tied to an objection and backed by competitor evidence.
- Pick the two shoppers: two very different ads for the same product. Highlight that the product is pixel-exact (SAM 3.1 cutout, guard ✅).
- Synthetic panel ranks variants live; weak ones are dropped.
- **Cost counter**: whole onboarding for this store: $X.XX.

## 1:40–2:20 — Proof
- Blind human test: "N people, personalized ad preferred X times out of 10."
- Panel vs humans agreement.
- Uplift curve on Criteo public data: same causal sales with Y% less budget.

## 2:20–2:45 — Autonomy
- Campaigns created on Meta (sandbox), budget capped.
- Weekly report + store fixes: "move the size guide above the fold".

## 2:45–3:00 — Close
Pitch line + Meta models used + "built in 10 days".

## Recording checklist
- [ ] 1080p, readable font sizes, no tokens or emails on screen
- [ ] Voice-over script timed
- [ ] Real run, no mock screens (or clearly labelled)
- [ ] Public link tested in a private window
