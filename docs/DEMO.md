# Demo script (3 minutes)

The video is a primary judging artefact. Show only what we built during the hackathon. Every number on screen is real and measured; every simulated number is labelled "simulated".

## 0:00–0:15 — Hook
"You pay Meta to find out your ad is bad." An app founder (design partner) about to launch a new ad. We drop it into a **simulated Instagram feed**, between the real ads its competitors are running right now.

## 0:15–0:45 — The feed reacts
Viewer agents built from real Meta reach data (age, gender, country) scroll the feed. Their reactions appear: hook noticed or not, believed or not, and **why not**: "I don't see what it costs", "looks like every other app". Score vs competitors: we lose.

## 0:45–1:25 — The agents fix it
- Counterfactual: SAM 3.1 isolates the first shot; without it, intent changes by X % (simulated).
- Creative director agent reads the objections and mutates the genome: new hook, proof first, new first frame (Muse Image), real app screen (SAM cutout, pixel guard ✅).
- v1 → v2 → v3: the score rises until we win the feed. Agent log on the side. **Cost counter: $X.XX.**

## 1:25–2:15 — Is the simulator right?
- Backtest on the partner's real past ads: "picks the better ad X times out of 10" vs baselines.
- Humans: agreement X %. Evolved ad preferred X % blind.
- Market: separates long-running from short-lived competitor ads (AUC X).
- The public bet: our predicted ranking posted on X on Oct 3x — and the real results.

## 2:15–2:45 — Product
- Arena: anyone drops their ad. Workspace: templates with evidence, weekly concepts, launch to Meta via Ads MCP with a hard cap.
- Distilled viewer on Meta open weights: X× cheaper.

## 2:45–3:00 — Close
"Meta automated the media buyer. We built the creative strategist — and the flight simulator." Meta models used. Built in 10 days.

## Recording checklist
- [ ] 1080p, readable fonts, no tokens, emails or partner-identifying data on screen
- [ ] Simulated numbers labelled
- [ ] Voice-over timed
- [ ] Real run, no mock screens
- [ ] Public link tested in a private window
