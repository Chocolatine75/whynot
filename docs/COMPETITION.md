## Answers to the obvious judge questions

**"Advantage+ already does personalized generation."**
Advantage+ personalises on *who* you are and optimises Meta-attributed conversions. whynot personalises on *why you didn't buy*, uses signals Meta doesn't see (on-site behaviour: size guide, shipping page, cart total), and measures causal lift. We feed Advantage+ better inputs rather than replacing it.

**"Criteo already does dynamic retargeting."**
Criteo shows the product you looked at. We answer the doubt that stopped you, and we don't spend on visitors who'd buy anyway.

**"Atria / Foreplay already analyse competitor ads."**
They stop at the brief. For us the brief is an input to a closed loop that generates, pre-tests, launches, learns and proves.

## Pitch line

*The others make you pay for customers you already had. We only reach the people an ad actually changes, answer what was holding them back, and prove it.*
