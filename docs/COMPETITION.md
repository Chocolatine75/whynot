# Competitive landscape

Execution is commoditised: generating ads, launching, budgeting, targeting and even answering customers are done by Meta or by many tools. What nobody does: **predict and explain how an ad will do among its real competitors, before spending, for small advertisers, with measured reliability — and fix it.**

## Answers to the obvious judge questions

**"Meta's ranking already predicts how ads perform."**
Yes — after you spend, and without telling you why. We're before spend, explainable, and we publish how often we're right. Advertisers then feed Andromeda better, more diverse creative, which is what Meta wants.

**"LLM viewers aren't real people."**
Correct, and we don't claim they are. We simulate cognitive reactions (understood, believed, objection, intent, preference), not scroll physics. We measure agreement with real ad performance, with humans and with market longevity, and we show those numbers.

**"Kantar / System1 already pre-test ads."**
For $4.5k–$11k per ad, in isolation, with a score. We place the ad among its real competitors, say why not, and fix it, for cents.

**"Why would Meta want this?"**
Better ads make a better feed for users; small advertisers who don't burn budget stay and grow; it showcases every Muse model, uses open weights at scale, and is built on Meta's Ad Library and Ads MCP.

**"Isn't it just an ad generator?"**
The generator is the cheap part. The product is the judge (the simulator), its measured reliability, and the explanation.
