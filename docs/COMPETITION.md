# Competitive landscape

Execution tools are everywhere: Meta automates delivery and tweaks creative, many tools generate ads, a few pre-test them for big brands. Nobody offers a small business **an agency that creates real new concepts, tests each one against its real competitors before spending, runs the account, and explains every decision — with measured reliability.**

## Answers to the obvious judge questions

**"Isn't this Meta's business AI assistant?"**
Meta's assistant works inside Meta, edits your existing assets and learns by spending your budget. whynot researches outside Meta (competitors' ads, reviews, your history), creates new concepts, tests each one before it costs anything, explains why, and plugs into Meta through the Ads MCP. Meta keeps bidding and delivery. We're complementary: we feed Andromeda better, more diverse creative.

**"Isn't it just an ad generator?" / "Isn't it just a pre-test?"**
Neither alone. A generator without a judge is one more AdCreative.ai; a judge without a generator is one more Kantar. The agency's engine is the loop: templates grounded in evidence generate, the simulator judges and explains, the agents evolve, real results recalibrate.

**"Ultra-personalised ads per person?"**
No: Meta audiences need hundreds of people and Meta decides who sees what. We create different concepts per customer profile; Meta delivers each to the people who respond.

**"LLM viewers aren't real people."**
Correct, and we don't claim they are. We simulate cognitive reactions (understood, believed, objection, intent, preference), not scroll physics. We measure agreement with real ad performance, with humans and with market longevity, and we show those numbers.

**"Why would Meta want this?"**
Better ads make a better feed for users; small advertisers who don't burn their budget stay and grow; it showcases every Muse model, uses open weights at scale, and is built on Meta's Ad Library and Ads MCP.
